## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **How much does renovation cost per square foot in Johor Bahru?**  
  In Johor Bahru, basic renovation ranges from RM150 to RM250 per square foot, mid-range RM250 to RM400, and premium fit-outs RM400 per square foot and above. Costs vary by materials, scope, and property type.
- **How much is a full renovation of a terrace house in JB?**  
  A full renovation of a standard JB terrace house (roughly 1,500 to 2,200 sq ft) typically ranges from RM350,000 to RM600,000, depending on the extent of works, materials, and whether structural changes are involved.
- **How much does it cost to renovate a condo in Johor Bahru?**  
  Condominium renovation in JB is usually more compact, ranging from RM50,000 to RM150,000 for a typical unit, depending on size and fit-out level. Management deposits and specific JMB requirements may add to the cost.
