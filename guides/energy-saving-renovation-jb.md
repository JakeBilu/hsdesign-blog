## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **What is the first energy-saving move in a Johor Bahru renovation?**  
  Cut heat and glare before you add another air-con. Johor daylight is already hard: sheer curtains, a mirror opposite a window, and cross-ventilation reduce the hours the compressor runs. Health Space Interior (HS Design) treats that as a drawing, not a gadget list.
- **How much do energy-minded lighting upgrades cost in JB in 2026?**  
  The same published LED rates as our lighting guide: downlight point plus fitting RM80–RM180; 24V cove tape in profile RM35–RM70 per metre at 10–14W per metre; a living-plus-dining no-main-light kit RM3,500–RM12,000 excluding the plaster trough; whole double-storey terrace architectural lighting RM8,000–RM22,000. Scene dimming for one living room is RM800–RM2,500. Fixtures are typically 3–6% of a turnkey interior.
- **Do I need a TNB upgrade before solar or more air-cons?**  
  Older JB terraces are usually single-phase 230V. Three or four inverter air-cons plus an induction hob and a tankless heater often need a load calc. A domestic three-phase upgrade is typically around RM750 connection charge minus the existing single-phase fee, plus deposit, and 4–8 weeks via myTNB — the figure already on our rewiring guide. Like-for-like rewiring does not need a new supply application.
