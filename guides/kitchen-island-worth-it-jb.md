## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **Is a kitchen island worth it in Malaysia?**  
  A kitchen island adds workspace, storage, and a social focal point. It's worth it if your kitchen has enough space (roughly 1m clearance on all sides) and you cook or entertain often. For small kitchens, it may not be practical.
- **How much does a kitchen island cost in Johor Bahru?**  
  A kitchen island in JB typically costs RM8,000 to RM25,000 depending on materials, size, and features like built-in sinks, hob, or seating. Custom cabinetry is the main cost driver.
- **What size kitchen is big enough for an island?**  
  Your kitchen should be at least 8-10 feet wide with about 1 metre of clearance around the island. In compact JB condos, a breakfast bar or peninsular might be a better use of space.
