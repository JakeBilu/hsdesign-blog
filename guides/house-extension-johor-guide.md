## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **How much does a house extension cost in Johor Bahru in 2026?**  
  Malaysia-wide extension rates in 2026 run RM150–RM250 per sq ft for a basic shell, RM200–RM350 per sq ft for standard work including structure and basic finishing, and RM300–RM450 per sq ft for premium finishes. In Johor Bahru, a typical single-storey rear extension of a 20ft x 15ft area (300 sq ft) lands at RM80,000–RM135,000 fully installed, while a second-storey addition typically costs RM150,000–RM250,000 or more depending on structural requirements and finishes.
- **Do I need a permit to extend my house in Johor?**  
  Yes. Any extension that increases floor area or changes the structure of a landed home in Johor requires building plan approval from the local council — MBJB for Johor Bahru, MBIP for Iskandar Puteri, or the relevant district council — submitted through the council's One-Stop-Centre (OSC) by a registered architect and, for structural works, a professional engineer. Extensions without approval risk a stop-work order, fines and an order to demolish. Budget RM1,000–RM5,000 for council submission and compliance fees and RM5,000–RM15,000 for professional (architect and engineer) fees.
- **How long does a house extension take in Malaysia?**  
  Plan for 2–4 months of council plan approval followed by 3–6 months of construction — around 6–10 months in total for a single-storey rear extension. A second-storey addition with heavier structural works, piling and transfer beams often stretches to 10–12 months. Weather during the monsoon season (November–February) also slows roof and wet works.
