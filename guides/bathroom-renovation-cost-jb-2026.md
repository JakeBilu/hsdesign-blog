## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **How much does a full bathroom renovation cost in Johor Bahru in 2026?**  
  In Johor Bahru in 2026, a cosmetic refresh (fixtures and tile-over without full plumbing replacement) typically runs RM7,500-RM11,000 per bathroom. A proper full gut-and-rebuild of a standard 45-60 sq ft bathroom—including hacking to bare slab, concealed PPR/copper piping, two-to-three-coat waterproofing with a 48-hour ponding test, R10/R11 porcelain tiles, tempered glass shower screen, and vanity cabinet—generally costs RM13,500-RM22,000, while luxury master bathrooms with wall-hung WC frames or freestanding tubs reach RM24,000-RM38,000+.
- **Why is tile-over-tile risky in older JB condos and terrace bathrooms?**  
  Laying new tiles directly over existing tiles seals over aged waterproofing membranes and hidden pipe joints without fixing them, raises the finished floor level toward the bedroom door, and reduces wet-area kerb height. If an old concealled joint leaks six months later, you must hack both tile layers at double the cost—and in a strata condo, the owner is liable for ceiling damage to the unit below.
- **How high should bathroom waterproofing go and how long is the ponding test?**  
  Waterproofing must cover the entire floor slab, turn up at least 300 mm on dry-zone walls, and reach at least 1,800 mm (preferably full ceiling height) inside the wet shower zone, with reinforcing fibreglass tape along all wall-to-floor corners and pipe collars. After curing, plug the floor trap and run a 24-to-48-hour ponding test with 25-50 mm of water before any screed or tile is laid.
