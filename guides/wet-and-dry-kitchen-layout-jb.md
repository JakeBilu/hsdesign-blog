## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **What suction power and duct size do I need for Chinese wok cooking in a Malaysian wet kitchen?**  
  Specify a dedicated external-exhaust cooker hood rated at 1,500 to 2,000+ m3/h (roughly 900 to 1,200 CFM) with a 150 mm to 180 mm (6 to 7 inch) rigid or semi-rigid duct routed straight outside. Avoid recirculating charcoal hoods and crushed flexible hoses, and keep a yard window slightly open during heavy frying for make-up air.
- **What is the minimum clearance around a dry kitchen island in a Johor Bahru home?**  
  Keep 1,000 mm to 1,200 mm between the island and the main cabinet run so two people can pass or open oven and dishwasher doors comfortably, and at least 900 mm on secondary sides. For seating, include a 250 mm to 300 mm knee overhang at a 850 mm to 900 mm standard worktop height. If total room width is under 2.4 m, use a wall-attached peninsula instead.
- **Should I choose full aluminium or multi-layer plywood cabinets for my wet and dry kitchens?**  
  Use full aluminium or 3G/4G aluminium-frame carcasses in the wet kitchen where water splashes, floor mopping, and humidity threaten timber boards and termites. In the dry kitchen, E0/E1 moisture-resistant multi-layer plywood gives tighter screw retention for heavy pantry pull-outs, quieter door closing, and warmer laminate or veneer styling at RM300–RM550 per running foot.
