## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **How much does it cost to renovate a staircase in Malaysia in 2026?**  
  For a standard double-storey terrace in Johor Bahru, 2026 market tiers are: a railing-only upgrade (mild steel grille replaced with stainless steel or glass, 8–10 running metres) RM3,000–RM8,000; a cosmetic refresh (tread cladding, railing repaint, new balusters) RM5,000–RM12,000; a moderate renovation (new treads and risers, new handrail and balusters, minor repair) RM12,000–RM22,000; a full conventional replacement RM20,000–RM40,000; and a bespoke open-riser floating stair with glass balustrade RM35,000–RM70,000+. Published rates are Klang Valley benchmarks; JB quotes typically land within about 10%.
- **Can you put wood on existing concrete stairs without rebuilding them?**  
  Yes — overlaying is the most common JB upgrade. Solid merbau treads (35 mm, tongued to the riser) over 16 steps run about RM5,500 installed, with ceramic or porcelain riser tiles adding roughly RM1,200. Old mosaic can be hacked off (about RM1,500 with disposal) or, if sound, overlaid directly. The worked mid-range example — timber treads, new tile risers, a powder-coated steel handrail with balusters, anti-slip nosing strips and repainting — totals around RM14,000 for one flight.
- **What are the staircase railing height and gap rules in Malaysia?**  
  The Uniform Building By-Laws require staircase guards to at least 900 mm above the pitch line, and balustrade openings must be small enough to stop a 125 mm sphere passing — the practical test for homes with toddlers. Older JB grille railings are often shorter than 900 mm or have climbable horizontal bars; replacing the balustrade is exactly where you fix that quietly while paying for aesthetics.
