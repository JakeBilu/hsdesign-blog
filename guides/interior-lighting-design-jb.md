## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **What is no-main-light interior design in Johor Bahru?**  
  No-main-light means the room is lit by layers — a cove or wall wash for ambient light, downlights or a magnetic track for tasks, and a few accent points — instead of one glaring ceiling rose in the centre. In 2026 JB living rooms this is the default hotel-style look. You still need enough lumens; you just stop putting them all in one 24W disc.
- **What colour temperature should I use in a JB home in 2026?**  
  Use 2700–3000K (warm white) in living rooms and bedrooms for evening comfort. Use 4000K (neutral) in dry kitchens, studies and bathrooms for task accuracy. Avoid mixing 3000K and 4000K in the same sightline, and avoid 5000–6500K ‘daylight’ lamps indoors — they look clinical under Johor’s already-bright daylight. Specify CRI 90+ so timber and skin tones do not go grey.
- **How much does interior lighting cost in Johor Bahru in 2026?**  
  Ballpark installed rates in 2026: LED downlights RM80–RM180 each including point and fitting; magnetic track RM180–RM350 per metre plus spots; 24V cove tape in an aluminium profile RM35–RM70 per metre; a living-plus-dining no-main-light package RM3,500–RM12,000 excluding the plaster trough. Scene dimming for one living room is typically RM800–RM2,500 extra. Fixtures are 3–6% of a turnkey interior if you stay with architectural LEDs rather than decorative chandeliers.
