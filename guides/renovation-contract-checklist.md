## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **How much deposit should I pay for a renovation in Johor Bahru in 2026?**  
  A reasonable 2026 JB deposit is 10–20% of the signed contract, or a capped booking fee of RM3,000–RM8,000 that converts into the first milestone. On a RM120,000 terrace turnkey that is RM12,000–RM24,000 — enough for drawings, mobilisation and first material orders, not enough to fund the whole job. Walk away from 40–50% upfront, cash-only deposits with no receipt, or any demand to pay the balance before a written defect list is signed.
- **What must a Malaysia renovation contract include?**  
  At minimum: parties’ legal names and SSM numbers, a dated itemised bill of quantities, drawings listed by revision, a payment schedule tied to site milestones, variation-order rules, a completion date with liquidated damages, defect-liability period (typically 6–12 months for interior works), insurance and CIDB/ST responsibilities, and a termination clause. A one-page quotation with a total only is not a contract.
- **What are typical renovation payment milestones in JB?**  
  A clean 2026 schedule is: 10–15% on signing (deposit); 20–25% after hacking and first-fix (electrical/plumbing in open walls); 25–30% after wet works, plaster and ceiling framing; 20–25% after carpentry, tiles and second-fix; 10% on practical completion after a joint defect walk; 5% retention released at the end of the defect-liability period. Pay for work that is done and inspected, not for a calendar date.
