## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **How much does a renovation cost in Johor Bahru in 2026?**  
  Work to the whole-project bands published on this site: a terrace-house interior sits at RM80,000–RM150,000 and a condo at RM40,000–RM80,000, with carpentry taking 30–35% of the budget, wet works and tiling 15–20%, electrical and plumbing 15%, painting and finishing 10%, fixtures 10%, and 10–15% held back as contingency. Per-square-foot rates depend on what the scope includes — check the cost-per-square-foot page before you multiply anything by your floor area.
- **Have renovation material prices gone up in 2026?**  
  Yes, on the official readings. DOSM's Building Cost Index for August 2026 puts Ordinary Portland cement at RM25.80 per 50 kg bag, up from RM23.33 in August 2024 — about 10.6% over two years. Mild steel round bars and Mycon 60 deformed bars average RM3,484.20 per tonne, and Johor's steel price index rose 6.4% year-on-year, one of the steepest increases in the country. A quotation written from 2024 material rates with no validity date or escalation clause is carrying that gap for you.
- **Do I pay 6% SST on my home renovation in Malaysia?**  
  Construction work services became taxable on 1 July 2025 at 6%, but the Royal Malaysian Customs Department's Guide on Construction Work Services (17 March 2026) is explicit: construction work on a building approved for residential use only is not levied, and its own worked example covers a homeowner renovating the interior design and electrical wiring of a residential house. The RM1.5 million registration threshold also excludes residential work. If your quotation adds 6% to a residential job, ask which line it sits under before you compare totals.
