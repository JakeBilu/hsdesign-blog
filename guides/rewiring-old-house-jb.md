## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **How much does it cost to rewire a house in Johor Bahru in 2026?**  
  In Johor Bahru in 2026, a full rewire typically costs RM6,000–RM12,000 for a 2–3 bedroom condo, RM8,000–RM15,000 for a single-storey terrace, RM12,000–RM22,000 for a double-storey terrace with concealed wiring, and RM18,000–RM35,000 for a semi-D or bungalow. A consumer-unit (DB board) upgrade alone is RM1,500–RM3,500. Concealed wiring runs 30–50% more than surface PVC conduit because of hacking and replastering.
- **Who is allowed to rewire a house in Malaysia?**  
  Electrical installation work must be carried out by a competent person registered with Suruhanjaya Tenaga (Energy Commission) under the Electricity Supply Act 1990 and Electricity Regulations 1994 — typically an ST-registered wireman working under a registered electrical contractor. Unlicensed work is illegal, voids insurance, and is the most common source of fire-risk wiring in older JB houses.
- **Do I need TNB approval to rewire my JB house?**  
  Like-for-like rewiring on the existing single-phase supply does not need a new TNB application, but the registered contractor still tests the installation and issues completion documents. Upgrading from single-phase to three-phase, increasing load, moving the meter, or installing an EV charger usually requires a myTNB application, TNB inspection, connection charges (around RM750 for a domestic three-phase upgrade, minus the existing single-phase fee) and 4–8 weeks of lead time.
