## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **How much do custom kitchen cabinets cost in Johor Bahru?**  
  In JB (2026 rates), custom kitchen cabinets are quoted per running foot of 2-tier cabinetry: melamine RM150–RM250, PVC foil RM180–RM280, acrylic RM250–RM400, plywood RM300–RM550 and solid wood RM550–RM900+. A typical 10–12 ft L-shaped kitchen with melamine cabinets and a quartz countertop lands around RM9,000–RM16,000, while a full acrylic fit-out runs RM16,000–RM30,000.
- **Are custom kitchen cabinets worth it compared to IKEA or ready-made sets?**  
  Custom cabinets win on fit, material choice and hardware quality — they are built to your exact wall dimensions, which matters in older JB terrace houses where walls and floors are rarely square. Ready-made options such as IKEA Tebrau or modular sets are cheaper upfront (a 10 ft IKEA kitchen starts around RM5,000–RM12,000 for cabinets) and faster (days instead of 3–6 weeks), but sizes are standard and installation and plumbing adjustments cost extra.
- **How long does custom kitchen cabinet fabrication take in JB?**  
  Plan for 3–6 weeks in Johor Bahru: 1–2 weeks for measurement and design confirmation, 2–4 weeks of factory fabrication, then 2–4 days on site for installation, sink and hob cut-outs and final adjustments. Quality shops fabricate only after the design is signed off, so changes after confirmation push the timeline out.
