## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **What is the real difference between sintered stone and quartz in Malaysia?**  
  Quartz is an engineered stone made of roughly 90% crushed quartz bound with 7-10% polymer resin and pigments, cured around 90-140 C. Sintered stone is 100% natural minerals compressed under high tonnage and kiln-fired above 1,200 C with zero resin. That single difference makes sintered stone impervious to hot pots and UV sunlight, whereas quartz resin can yellow, scorch, or crack under thermal shock.
- **How much do sintered stone and quartz cost in Johor Bahru in 2026?**  
  For standard 600 mm deep kitchen countertops installed in Johor Bahru in 2026, quartz runs RM200-RM350 per running foot (15-20 mm), while genuine sintered stone runs RM350-RM600 per running foot (12 mm or 20 mm slab, excluding decorative waterfall mitres). For full-height living room TV feature walls, 6-12 mm large-format sintered slabs typically range from RM85 to RM160 per sq ft including structural plywood/steel backing and mitred TV console edges.
- **Does sintered stone crack or chip more easily than quartz on kitchen edges?**  
  Yes on sharp unsupported edges. Because sintered stone is ultra-hard and sintered without elastic resin, it has higher surface scratch resistance (Mohs 6-7) but lower impact toughness at thin 12 mm edges and sink cut-outs. To prevent stress cracks, insist on full-sheet carcass support, CNC-polished 5-10 mm rounded inner corners around hob and sink cut-outs, and a double-thickened or mitred apron edge instead of exposed 12 mm sharp arris.
