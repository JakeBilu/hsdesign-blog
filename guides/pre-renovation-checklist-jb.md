## Key 2026 Johor Bahru Engineering Specifications & Takeaways

### 1. Confirm Permits & Approvals
Check MBJB/JMB approvals and structural permits before demolition. Condos need management approval and deposits.

### 2. Set a Realistic Budget
Plan with a budget breakdown and 10–15% contingency. Check cost per square foot to gauge expectations.

### 3. Verify Your Contractor & Designer
Ask the right questions, check references, and confirm whether it's turnkey or design-only.

### 4. Inspect Structure & Defects
Do a defect check and structural assessment. Old houses may need extra attention.

### 5. Plan the Timeline
Confirm a realistic schedule, including the demolition phase which can take months. Arrange accommodation if needed.

## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **What should I check before starting a renovation in Johor Bahru?**  
  Check permits (MBJB/JMB), set a realistic budget with contingency, verify your contractor's credentials, inspect the property structure and defects, and have a clear design and timeline before work begins.
- **How do I prepare my Johor Bahru home for renovation?**  
  Empty the space, protect belongings, plan a temporary place to stay (especially during demolition), secure utilities, and confirm access for contractors and material deliveries.
- **What permits are needed before renovating in JB?**  
  Landed homes may need MBJB/MBIP approval for structural changes; condos need JMB (management) approval and often a renovation deposit. Confirm before demolition.
