## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **How much does a hidden door and fluted feature wall cost in Johor Bahru in 2026?**  
  In Johor Bahru in 2026, a flush frameless hidden door with an aluminium sub-frame and 3-axis concealed hinges typically costs RM2,400 to RM4,200 per set, rising to RM3,200 to RM5,500 for full-height fluted-cladded doors with hydraulic soft-closing hardware. WPC fluted feature wall cladding generally runs RM28 to RM48 per sq ft installed, while real wood veneer slats range from RM45 to RM85 per sq ft.
- **Why do hidden doors start sagging or scraping floor tiles within a year?**  
  The two main causes are undersized hinges and oversized door-leaf weight. Full-height 2,400 mm doors cladded with thick MDF or timber slats can exceed 55 kg; fitting only two light hinges causes the top hinge screws to pull loose in soft timber frames, dropping the latch edge onto the tiles. Specify at least three or four heavy-duty 3-axis adjustable concealed hinges anchored to an aluminium or seasoned hardwood frame.
- **Should I use hydraulic closer hinges or push-to-open latches for a hidden bathroom door?**  
  Avoid push-to-open spring latches on guest bathrooms or toilets. Push latches lack a reliable privacy lock, wear out faster from damp bathroom airflow pressure, and can bounce open unexpectedly. Use a flush recessed pull or slim handle paired with a magnetic mortise privacy latch and either concealed 3D hinges with an overhead concealed closer or heavy-duty hydraulic hinges rated for the door weight.
