## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **What is health space design?**  
  Health space design creates homes that support physical and mental wellbeing — considering indoor air quality, natural light, acoustics, thermal comfort, biophilic elements, and non-toxic materials.
- **How can I make my Johor Bahru home healthier?**  
  Improve ventilation and air quality (consider formaldehyde removal in new homes), maximise natural light, use low-VOC materials, add plants, improve acoustics, and design for calm, clutter-free spaces.
- **What materials are best for a healthy home in Malaysia?**  
  Choose low-VOC paints, formaldehyde-free boards, natural fibres, and breathable materials. Avoid materials that trap humidity, which is common in Malaysia's tropical climate.
