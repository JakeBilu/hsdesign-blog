## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **How many air conditioners does a Johor Bahru bedroom need?**  
  It depends on the room, not a fixed number. Floor area, ceiling height, window direction and glass area, number of occupants and heat from appliances all change the sizing, so ask the supplier to state the room conditions behind the recommendation and compare like for like. A west-facing room with large glass usually needs more attention to shading and sealing than to a larger unit alone.
- **Can the installer drill anywhere for the refrigerant pipes?**  
  No. Drilling through structural members, waterproof layers or a neighbour's space can create damage far beyond the pipe run, and some schemes require approval. Ask for the planned route, the pipe length, insulation and sealing method in the written scope, and have the structural and waterproofing implications checked before the work starts. Penetration points that are not properly sealed later become leaks.
- **Do I need an electrician before booking the air conditioner?**  
  Yes, at least for the assessment. Older properties may not have the circuit capacity, earthing or protective devices that a continuously running unit needs, and the assessment is far cheaper before the equipment is on site. Ask the electrician to check the supply and state in writing whether an existing circuit is sufficient or a dedicated one is required, and keep that in your installation file.
