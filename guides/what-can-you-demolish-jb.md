## Key 2026 Johor Bahru Engineering Specifications & Takeaways

### Question 1: Can I Knock Down a Wall in my JB Home?
Yes, but only non-load-bearing internal partition walls. These thin walls separate rooms and carry no structural weight. Structural (load-bearing) walls cannot be removed without an engineer's approval.

### Question 2: What is a Load-Bearing Wall?
A load-bearing wall supports the weight of the roof, upper floor, or structure above. Removing it causes old houses especially to crack, sag, or settle dangerously.

### Question 3: Do I Need a Permit to Demolish in JB?
For structural changes to landed homes, you may need MBJB or MBIP approval. For condos, get building management (JMB) approval, which may require a renovation deposit.

### Question 4: Which Structures Should Never Be Demolished?
Columns, beams, staircases, lift shafts, and walls under roof trusses are structural. Removing them risks electrical and wiring issues, sagging, and collapse. Always get an engineer's report.

### Question 5: How Do I Know Which Walls Are Safe?
Check the building floor plan for thick structural walls, look for columns or beams, and have a professional designer or engineer verify. When in doubt, do not demolish.

## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **Can I knock down a wall in my Johor Bahru home?**  
  You can knock down non-load-bearing, internal partition walls safely. Load-bearing (structural) walls cannot be removed without engineering approval and heavy reinforcement. Always confirm with a structural engineer before any demolition.
- **What is a load-bearing wall?**  
  A load-bearing (structural) wall supports the weight of the roof, upper floors, or building structure above it. Removing it without proper support can cause walls to crack, ceilings to sag, or worse. Never demolish without a professional assessment.
- **Do I need a permit to demolish walls in Johor Bahru?**  
  In Johor Bahru, structural changes to landed homes may require approval from the local council (MBJB or MBIP). For condominiums, you need approval from the building management (JMB), and some require a renovation deposit.
