## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **Can every balcony be enclosed?**  
  Do not assume so. Establish the proposed design’s feasibility, relevant authority and applicable property-management requirements for the actual home before ordering glazing or fixed coverings.
- **Does new tiling prove a balcony leak is fixed?**  
  No. Record the symptom, obtain an appropriate assessment and agree the repair and verification method. A new finish alone does not establish that the underlying cause has been resolved.
- **Can I put heavy planters anywhere?**  
  Have the actual proposed loading and arrangement reviewed by an appropriate qualified professional. Keep drainage and maintenance access open; do not estimate a safe load from floor area or photographs.
