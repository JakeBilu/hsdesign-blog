## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **Quartz vs granite vs solid surface — which kitchen countertop for Johor Bahru in 2026?**  
  Quartz is the JB default: stain-resistant, consistent colour, no sealing. Granite is tougher under a hot wok and cheaper per foot, but it needs periodic sealing. Solid surface (Corian-type 人造石) welds into seamless joins and can have scratches sanded out. A common 2026 split is quartz on the dry-kitchen island and granite or solid surface in the wet kitchen.
- **How much does a kitchen countertop cost in Johor Bahru in 2026?**  
  Installed 2026 JB rates per running foot, matching our custom-cabinet guide: laminate RM60–RM120; granite RM150–RM250; quartz RM200–RM350; solid surface RM250–RM400; sintered stone RM350–RM600. A 10 ft quartz run is about RM2,000–RM3,500; the same length in sintered stone roughly doubles. Countertops plus cabinets are typically 25–35% of a full renovation.
- **Which countertop survives a JB wet kitchen and wok cooking?**  
  Granite and sintered stone take a hot wok and a kettle better than quartz or solid surface. Quartz resins can scorch or discolour under a pot parked on the slab. In a wet-dry layout, put the heat-proof slab at the hob and the prettier quartz on the dry island guests see.
