## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **Why does the same paint colour look different at home?**  
  Daylight, lamps, neighbouring finishes, surface texture and sheen affect how a colour appears. Test the intended product and finish beside your actual floor and cabinetry under both day and night lighting before approval.
- **Can I approve a wall colour from a phone photograph?**  
  Use photographs to record the sample identity, not as the sole colour reference. Camera exposure and screen settings vary. Confirm the physical sample in the room and record the manufacturer, code, product and finish.
- **Will anti-mould paint solve a damp wall?**  
  Do not use a decorative coating as a substitute for finding and correcting moisture entry. Investigate recurring stains, peeling and dampness, agree the repair and preparation scope, and follow the chosen coating system's requirements.
