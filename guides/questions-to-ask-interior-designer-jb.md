## Key 2026 Johor Bahru Engineering Specifications & Takeaways

### 1. What Exactly Are You Charging For?
Understanding fees is crucial. Ask for a transparent breakdown of design, materials, labour, and management — this prevents hidden costs later.

### 2. Is It Design-Only or Turnkey?
A turnkey service is hands-off — one party manages everything. Design-only gives you more control but you coordinate contractors yourself.

### 3. How Long Will My Project Take?
Get a realistic timeline. Structural changes and demolition can take months, so confirm the schedule upfront.

### 4. Will You Supervise the Site?
Site supervision ensures work matches the design. Ask how often they visit and whether supervision is included, especially if you're abroad.

### 5. Can I See Your Portfolio and References?
Review portfolio work similar to your project. Ask for references from past clients in JB.

### 6. What Permits or Approvals Are Needed?
Confirm who handles MBJB/JMB approvals and structural matters. The designer should guide you.

## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **What questions should I ask an interior designer?**  
  Ask about pricing and quotes, turnaround time, whether it's a turnkey or design-only service, previous portfolio, site supervision, permits and management approval, warranty, and how revisions are handled.
- **What is a turnkey interior design service?**  
  A turnkey service manages the entire project from design to completion — including demolition, construction, carpentry, and coordination — so you receive a finished space without managing contractors yourself.
- **How much do interior designers charge in Johor Bahru?**  
  Fees vary: some charge a design fee, others bake it into the turnkey package. Ask for a transparent itemised quote covering design, materials, labour, and management fees.
