## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **Is renovating always cheaper than moving in Johor Bahru?**  
  No universal answer follows from a renovation total alone. Compare the actual scope, the specific alternative home, transaction and relocation costs, finance commitments and unresolved condition risks. Keep initial cash and ongoing costs separate.
- **What should I check before asking for a renovation price?**  
  Define the household problem, document the existing layout and condition, and identify essential repairs. Obtain appropriate assessment for suspected structural, electrical or leak issues, then request an itemized scope with inclusions and exclusions.
- **Can I assume an extension will give me enough space to stay?**  
  Treat extra space as conditional until the actual site, proposed work, relevant authority and applicable management requirements have been checked. Ask the appropriate professional for a written feasibility conclusion and keep a fallback layout.
