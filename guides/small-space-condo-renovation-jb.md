## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **How do you make a small Malaysian condo feel bigger?**  
  Use light colours, maximise vertical storage, choose multifunctional furniture (fold-away beds, wall desks), minimise partitions, and maximise natural light. Built-in storage that uses every inch is key.
- **What furniture is best for a small condo?**  
  Multifunctional and space-saving furniture works best — sofa beds, storage ottomans, wall-mounted desks, and modular shelving. Custom built-ins that fit the space perfectly are ideal.
- **How much does a small condo renovation cost in JB?**  
  A small JB condo renovation typically ranges from RM30,000 to RM80,000 depending on scope. Focus on smart storage and multifunctional built-ins to maximise every ringgit.
