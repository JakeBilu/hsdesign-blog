## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **When should I start renovating my JB condo ahead of the RTS Link opening?**  
  Start planning 12 to 16 weeks before your target move-in or tenant handover date. With the JB-Singapore RTS Link targeting passenger operations in February 2027 following systems integration tests, you need time for developer defect rectification, JMB/MC renovation approval, carpentry fabrication (4 to 6 weeks), and on-site installation.
- **Should I finish the developer defect inspection before starting renovation?**  
  Yes. Under Malaysia's standard Schedule H Sale and Purchase Agreement, new stratified homes carry a 24-month Defect Liability Period (DLP) from vacant possession. If your contractor hacks tiles, alters waterproofing, or modifies M&E lines before the developer logs and rectifies defects, the developer can void responsibility for the affected areas.
- **How much does it cost to renovate a condo in Johor Bahru in 2026?**  
  A practical 2026 condo renovation in Johor Bahru typically falls within RM40,000 to RM80,000 for a 2-bedroom unit with essential built-ins, lighting, aircon piping, and painting, while compact 1-bedroom fit-outs start around RM32,000–RM45,000 and larger 3-bedroom full custom carpentry packages run RM70,000–RM105,000+. Always hold an additional 10–15% contingency.
