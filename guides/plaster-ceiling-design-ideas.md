## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **How much does a plaster ceiling cost in Johor Bahru in 2026?**  
  In Johor Bahru in 2026, a simple perimeter box or skimmed drop typically costs RM12–RM22 per sq ft of treated ceiling, a boxed cove with a lighting trough RM18–RM35 per sq ft, and a multi-level feature tray RM28–RM50 per sq ft including labour and standard gypsum board. A living-plus-dining box of about 400 sq ft lands at RM5,000–RM10,000, or RM8,000–RM16,000 with cove LED. A master-bedroom drop with cove is usually RM2,500–RM6,000. LED strips, drivers and magnetic tracks are extras.
- **Is gypsum board or wet plaster better for a JB ceiling?**  
  Gypsum board (plasterboard on a metal frame) is the 2026 default for feature ceilings in Johor because it is faster, lighter and easier to form lighting troughs and beam wraps. Wet cement plaster on the slab is for making-good and a flat skim, not for coves. Use moisture-resistant board near bathrooms and dry kitchens. Fibrous plaster or GRG is for curves and is 2–3 times the labour of a straight box. PVC or decorative cornice alone is not a substitute for a designed drop.
- **How much ceiling height do I lose with a plaster ceiling in a JB condo?**  
  Most JB condos have a slab-to-slab height of about 2,590–2,740 mm (8 ft 6 in to 9 ft). A perimeter box should drop 80–150 mm; a lighting trough needs about 100–120 mm clear for the aluminium profile, diffuser and service access. Keep the centre of the room at slab height or a shallow 30–50 mm skim so the space does not feel compressed. Terrace houses with 3 m+ floor-to-floor can take a deeper tray. Always measure the finished floor and the air-con cassette or fan before locking the drop.
