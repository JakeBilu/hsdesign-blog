## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **What does your Turnkey service include?**  
  Our turnkey service covers everything from A to Z. It includes initial layout and 3D conceptualization, space planning, authority submissions (if needed), material sourcing, hacking and wet works, carpentry, lighting, electrical wiring, and final soft furnishing. We manage the entire timeline and quality control, so you simply take the keys and move in.
- **Do you offer design-only or site supervision services?**  
  Yes. If you already have a contractor you trust, we can provide comprehensive design blueprints and realistic 3D visualizations for them to follow. We can also act as an independent site supervisor to ensure your contractor executes the design flawlessly and adheres to our quality standards.
- **Which areas do you cover?**  
  We primarily serve residential and commercial properties across Johor Bahru (JB), including areas like Skudai, Johor Jaya, Mount Austin, Desa Tebrau, Eco Botanic, Setia Tropika, and Puteri Harbour. For projects outside JB, feel free to contact us to discuss logistics.
