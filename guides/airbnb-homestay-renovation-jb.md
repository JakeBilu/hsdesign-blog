## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **How much does an Airbnb or homestay renovation cost in Johor Bahru in 2026?**  
  There is no separate Airbnb package. A guest-ready unit uses the same interior ranges we already publish: a condominium typically RM40,000–RM80,000, or RM60,000–RM120,000 when the scope is a fuller remote turnkey; a terrace typically RM80,000–RM150,000 (some fuller landed jobs RM80,000–RM200,000). Bathrooms RM8,000–RM15,000 including membrane; a hotel-style master cove RM2,500–RM6,000. Nightly yield is occupancy times rate — it is not a renovation quote.
- **What should a JB short-stay interior include?**  
  A hotel-style bedroom (2700–3000K layered light, no single harsh rose), a dry kitchen that photographs, lockable storage for the owner, and wet works that survive weekly turnovers: waterproofing, branded soft-close hardware, SPC or tiles instead of solid timber. Health Space Interior (HS Design) specs that stack on the same drawing as a family reno — the difference is durability and a listing photo, not a castle theme.
- **Do I need JMB or council approval to run a homestay in a JB condo?**  
  Ask the joint management body or management corporation before you buy the sofa. Many JB condos restrict short-stay or require written consent and a renovation deposit with a registered contractor — the same JMB form we already mention for rewiring. MBJB or MBIP papers apply if you hack a wall or change the facade (permit fees on our permit page are RM100–RM300, two to four weeks). Short-stay licensing is a separate legal question from the fit-out; we do not invent a licence price here.
