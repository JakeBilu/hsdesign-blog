## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **What makes a kids room safe in a Johor Bahru home in 2026?**  
  Round or eased carpentry edges, shuttered sockets on the first-fix plan, window restrictors on upper floors, soft-close hinges so doors do not slam fingers, and no bunk without a proper rail and a clear fall zone. Health Space Interior (HS Design) treats young children the same way our hiring guide does: they shape edges, ventilation and fire-safety more than taste does.
- **How much does a kids bedroom renovation cost in Johor Bahru in 2026?**  
  A quality built-in wardrobe sits in the same RM3,000–RM10,000 band we quote for a carpenter-built island. Floor-to-ceiling joinery is priced like other carpentry: laminated board from about RM250 per running foot up to RM700+ for solid wood or imported plywood. A flat plaster drop in a kids room is RM1,200–RM2,500. One second bedroom is a slice of a condo interior (RM40,000–RM80,000) or a terrace (RM80,000–RM150,000), not a separate package.
- **How do I design a JB kids room that grows from toddler to teen?**  
  Keep the built-in wardrobe and the bed wall; change the loose pieces. A floor-to-ceiling carcass with adjustable shelves outlasts a themed flat-pack. A wall desk can be remounted as they grow — our home-office note uses 73–75 cm and at least 60 cm depth for computer work, which is the teen setting. Skip cartoon murals baked into the joinery; paint is cheaper to redo than a carved castle headboard.
