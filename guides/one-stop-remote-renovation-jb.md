- **Can I renovate my JB property remotely while living in Singapore?**  
  Yes. A one-stop interior design firm in Johor Bahru handles design, quotations, material ordering, site supervision, progress reports and final handover, so you only need to review weekly photo and video updates. Contracts can be signed digitally or in person during weekend visits.
