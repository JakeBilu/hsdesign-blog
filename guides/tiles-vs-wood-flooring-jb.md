## Key 2026 Johor Bahru Engineering Specifications & Takeaways

### Porcelain Tiles: The Wet-Area Champion
Tiles are water-resistant, durable, and cool. They're ideal for kitchens and bathrooms, where moisture and spills are common.

### Wood & Wood-Look: Warmth With Caution
Engineered wood and wood-look laminate add warmth, but solid wood can warp in humidity. Use them in dry living and bedroom areas.

### Cost Comparison in JB (per sq ft)
Tiles RM5–30 · Engineered wood RM10–25 · Solid wood RM30+. Your budget and room use decide the best value.

### A Health-Space Perspective
Consider allergens and indoor air quality. Tiles are easy to keep allergen-free; choose low-VOC wood options for better health spaces.

## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **Is tile or wood flooring better in Malaysia?**  
  For hot, humid Malaysia, porcelain tiles are more practical in high-moisture areas (kitchen, bathrooms, wet areas). Engineered wood or wood-look laminate is a popular, cost-effective choice for dry living areas.
- **Which flooring is more durable in a Johor Bahru home?**  
  Porcelain tiles are highly durable and water-resistant. Vinyl planks and engineered wood resist humidity better than solid wood, which can warp in Malaysia's climate.
- **How much does flooring cost per square foot in JB?**  
  Basic tiles start around RM5-15 per sq ft installed, mid-range porcelain RM15-30, and engineered wood or laminate RM10-25. Premium imported options cost more.
