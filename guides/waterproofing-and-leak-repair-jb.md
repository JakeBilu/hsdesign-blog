## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **How much does waterproofing cost in Johor Bahru?**  
  In JB (2026 rates), cementitious waterproofing costs RM6–RM12 per square foot applied, and polyurethane (PU) liquid membrane costs RM12–RM20 per square foot. Re-waterproofing and retiling a typical bathroom costs RM2,500–RM5,000 as a standalone job, or RM8,000–RM15,000 when done inside a full bathroom renovation.
- **How long does bathroom waterproofing take?**  
  A proper bathroom waterproofing job takes 5–7 days in Johor Bahru: 1–2 days to hack and prepare the substrate, 1–2 days to apply 2–3 coats of membrane with 24–48 hours of curing between coats, then a 24–48 hour ponding water test before tiling. Skipping the curing or the water test is how leaks come back.
- **How long does waterproofing last in Malaysia's climate?**  
  Quality PU liquid membrane systems last 5–10 years, and cementitious systems 5+ years under Malaysian weather. JB's high rainfall (2,000–2,500 mm a year) and humidity shorten the life of cheap products. Reputable contractors offer a 5-year workmanship warranty on membrane systems — get it in writing.
