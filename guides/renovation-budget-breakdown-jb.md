## Key 2026 Johor Bahru Engineering Specifications & Takeaways

### Typical JB Budget Allocation
Carpentry & cabinetry 30–35% · Wet works & tiling 15–20% · Electrical & plumbing 15% · Paint & finishing 10% · Fixtures 10% · Contingency 10–15%.

### Why Kitchen and Cabinetry Cost the Most
Custom kitchen cabinetry, wardrobes, and built-ins make up the largest share. Materials and eco-friendly options vary the cost.

### The Contingency Fund You Can't Skip
Set aside 10–15% for unexpected costs like hidden wall damage or structural surprises. This keeps your project on track.

### Budget by Property Type
A terrace house needs more for carpentry; a condo tends to focus on compact storage and space-saving.

## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **How should I allocate my renovation budget in Johor Bahru?**  
  A common JB allocation: 30-35% carpentry and cabinetry, 15-20% wet works and tiling, 15% electrical and plumbing, 10% paint and finishing, 10% fixtures, and 10-15% contingency.
- **Why is a contingency fund important in renovation?**  
  Unexpected issues like hidden wall damage, structural surprises, or material price changes are common. Setting aside 10-15% avoids stopping mid-project or resorting to low-quality shortcuts.
- **How much does kitchen cabinetry cost in a JB renovation?**  
  Kitchen and custom cabinetry is typically the single biggest line item, often 30-35% of the total budget, depending on materials and complexity.
