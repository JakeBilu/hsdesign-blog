## Key 2026 Johor Bahru Engineering Specifications & Takeaways

### Phase-by-Phase Renovation Timeline
A typical JB renovation follows these phases. Times are estimates and vary with property condition and project scope.

### Planning & Design (2–4 weeks)
Space planning, material selection, and design drawings. This stage is where budgeting errors usually happen, so lock down decisions early.

### Demolition & Structural Work (6–12 weeks)
Removing old finishes, hacking walls, and structural adjustments. This is often the longest phase — for major structural changes, confirm permits beforehand and expect 6 to 12 weeks.

### Electrical & Plumbing (1–2 weeks)
Rewiring and re-piping, especially for smart home readiness.

### Tiling & Wet Works (2–3 weeks)
Flooring, waterproofing, and bathroom works. Allow curing time for tiling — rushing this stage causes cracks later.

### Carpentry & Joinery (2–4 weeks)
Custom cabinetry, wardrobe, and built-ins. This is the phase most affected by cabinetry lead times.

## Quick Engineering FAQ

- **How long does a full renovation take in Johor Bahru?**  
  A full home renovation in Johor Bahru typically takes 4 to 8 months. This covers design and planning, demolition and structural work, electrical and plumbing, wet works, carpentry, painting, and final handover inspection. Structural changes and demolition alone can take 6 to 12 weeks.
- **Can I stay in my home during renovation?**  
  It is not advisable to stay in your home during a full renovation, especially during demolition and wet works, due to dust, noise, and safety hazards. Phased or partial renovations may allow some areas to remain livable.
- **Do I need a permit to renovate my home in Johor Bahru?**  
  In Johor Bahru, high-rise condominiums require approval from the building management (JMB) before renovation. For landed properties, substantial structural changes may require approval from the local council (MBJB or MBIP).
